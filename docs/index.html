MELD emotion prototype

A take-home submission for the ML route: each MELD clip goes through Whisper for its words, three frozen encoders for features, a small head trained in Rust for one of seven emotions, and LFM2.5 for a short reply. The whole inference path holds about 2.8 billion learned parameters, under the brief's 6 billion.

How the head is trained The approach, the model library with a 6B combo builder, the track comparison, the A40 results and the training steps. Benchmark results Sortable tables from the A40 run: speech to text, frozen encoders with trained heads, reply-writer speed and vision-language models. Source and steps to reproduce The repository and its README: environments, cleaned labels, Whisper transcripts, features, Rust training and the live player.
Built on MELD, the Multimodal EmotionLines Dataset.
